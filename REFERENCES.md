# Sources and attribution

## Original problem and foundational identities

1. **Wadim Zudilin.** *On a family of polynomials related to ζ(2,1)=ζ(3).* First arXiv submission 29 April 2015; manuscript dated 24 April 2015; v2 dated 31 March 2017. Section 4, equation (8), Lemmas 3–4, and the concluding root-location observation supply the family, recurrence, reflection identity, and intended real-parameter target. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.07696) · [Inspected v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.07696v2#S4).
2. **Wadim Zudilin.** Same title, in J. I. Burgos Gil, K. Ebrahimi-Fard, and H. Gangl (eds.), *Periods in Quantum Field Theory and Arithmetic*, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 314 (2020), 621–630. First online 14 March 2020. DOI [10.1007/978-3-030-37031-2_22](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-37031-2_22). This is the later publication venue, not the date of the original observation. [Publisher metadata](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-37031-2_22).
3. **Howard S. Cohl (editor), with the Section 7 problem posed by Wadim Zudilin.** *Report from the Open Problems Session at OPSFA13.* SIGMA 12 (2016), 071. Session held 4 June 2015; report published 21 July 2016. Section 7, pages 10–11, records this family and the experimental negative-root observation, alongside separate A-polynomial questions. [Published report](https://sigma-journal.com/2016/071/sigma16-071.pdf#page=10).
4. **Wadim Zudilin.** June 2015 lecture slides, slide 13, corroborating the all-real-parameter formulation. [Author's lecture material](https://carmamaths.org/meetings/mathsandcomputation/pdfs/mathscomp2015-zudilin.pdf#page=13).

## Related subsequent work

5. **Wen-Gao Long, Dan Dai, Yu-Tian Li, and Xiang-Sheng Wang.** *Asymptotics of orthogonal polynomials with asymptotic Freud-like weights.* Studies in Applied Mathematics **144 (2020), no. 2, 133–163**, first published online 6 December 2019. DOI [10.1111/sapm.12291](https://doi.org/10.1111/sapm.12291). The inspected author PDF records receipt on 6 March 2019 and revision on 1 November 2019; the issue and publication dates are confirmed by the [publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/sapm.12291). Its introduction relates the B-polynomials to continuous dual Hahn polynomials with parameters depending on t and/or n; its subsequent fixed-parameter asymptotic results do not directly prove this B-family theorem. [Author-hosted PDF](https://userweb.ucs.louisiana.edu/~xxw6637/papers/SAPM2020.pdf).
6. **Erik Koelink, Pablo Román, and Wadim Zudilin.** *A partial-sum deformation for a family of orthogonal polynomials.* arXiv:2409.00261 (2024; v2, 2025), Indagationes Mathematicae 36 (2025), no. 6, 1745–1761. DOI [10.1016/j.indag.2025.04.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.indag.2025.04.007). This concerns a different partial-sum family and a separate threshold conjecture, not the 2015 B-polynomial assertion. [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.00261v2).
